Changeloom is published by <developer name, as shown on Google Play>. You can delete your account
and the data linked to it at any time.

## Delete in the app

1. Open Changeloom and sign in.
2. Go to **Profile**, scroll to the bottom and tap **Delete account**.
3. Confirm. You may be asked to sign in again.

Your data is deleted right away, and you are signed out.

## Delete by email

If you can't use the app, email **<support email>** from the address you sign in with, with the
subject "Delete my account". We delete the account within <N> days and reply to confirm.

## What is deleted

- Your account: your email address and sign-in ID.
- The topics you follow.
- Your read history and saved (bookmarked) stories.
- The push-notification tokens for your devices.
- Your topic requests.

## What is kept

- Server logs have no email addresses or account IDs. They are kept for <log retention, e.g. 30 days> and then deleted.
- Database backups may still hold your data for up to <backup/restore window> after deletion. After that, it is gone.
- Stories and topics aren't personal data. They stay in the shared catalog, including topics created from requests.
